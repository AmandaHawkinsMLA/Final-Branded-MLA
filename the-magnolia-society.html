Girls Mentorship

The Magnolia Society

Planted. Rooted. Rising.

A mentorship initiative for girls and young women in Mississippi — built to develop character, confidence, community, and the quiet strength that carries a woman through every season of life.

"She is clothed with strength and dignity; she can laugh at the days to come."Proverbs 31:25

Every magnolia was planted before it bloomed.

The Magnolia Society is Mississippi Life Alliance's girls mentorship program — a community of young women learning what it means to grow with grace, lead with integrity, and show up for one another.

Through mentorship, programming, events, and the Magnolia Rising pageant experience, girls are given space to discover who they are and what they carry.

Get Involved

Mentorship Circles

Small group mentorship experiences built around character, confidence, identity, and real conversations that matter.

Leadership Development

Public speaking, service opportunities, civic engagement, and skills that build women who lead from within.
