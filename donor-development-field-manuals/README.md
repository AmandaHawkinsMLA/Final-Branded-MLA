# Porchlight Donor Development Field Manuals

**Mississippi Life Alliance / The Porchlight Initiative**  
**Working system - November 2026**

This repository index tracks the printable donor-development manual set created for Porchlight. The print set uses the current Porchlight publication direction: cream/ivory editorial language, deep Mississippi navy, rooted green, antique-gold detail, strong serif hierarchy, restrained tree branding, and a sea-moss footer treatment.

## Readability standard

All working manuals use **13 pt body copy**, deliberately two points larger than a conventional 11 pt business-document default. Tables, notes, headers, and footers are also enlarged from their usual defaults.

## Master order of operations

1. Clean and segment donor data; assign relationship owners and next actions.
2. Build the print room and lock current brand, contact, legal, and program standards.
3. Fix acknowledgment and first-90-day stewardship before launching more acquisition.
4. Build impact cards and quarterly reporting so donors can see what happened.
5. Build due-diligence, benefactor, CPA/advisor, and corporate materials.
6. Build verified major-gift opportunities and ways-to-give pathways.
7. Launch recurring-donor cultivation and year-end reporting.
8. Measure retention, recurring growth, follow-up completion, reactivation, and channel performance every month.

## Research anchors used

- **FEP Q2 2026:** dollars up 6.6% year over year while donor count fell 1.6%; donors who had given 7+ times the prior year were retained at 90% versus 14.4% of one-time donors.
- **M+R Benchmarks 2026:** direct-mail revenue rose 9% in 2025; active-donor mail averaged $4.51 in revenue per $1 spent; monthly giving represented 27% of online revenue.
- **Bloomerang Mission Retainable:** 65% of donors wanted regular impact updates; 24% reported stopping support over insufficient transparency.
- **Bloomerang 2026 Giving Signals:** 69% cited financial/program transparency as a top effectiveness signal.
- **Blackbaud First Impressions:** trust, reputation, ease of giving, wise use of funds, and helping someone now were major repeat-gift factors for 75%+ of spontaneous first-time donors.
- **Fidelity Charitable 2026:** 78% of donors identified a financial advisor or CPA/tax professional as a trusted source for charitable-giving guidance.
- **AFP:** acknowledgment within 48-72 hours is current best practice; earlier AFP guidance cites research associating a thank-you within 48 hours with substantially stronger repeat giving.

## Non-negotiable production gates

- No impact statistic goes to print without a traceable source.
- No unit-cost statement goes to print without verified cost math.
- No public family story uses identifying details without documented permission.
- No donor-facing tax/legal claim is improvised.
- No major meeting ends without an owner, next action, and date.
- No new donor asset is considered finished until a physical print proof passes.
- Stewardship should outweigh solicitation; the working discipline is at least three relationship-building touches for every ask.
- Families are neighbors, never props.
