Donation Tips

✓ Gently used clothing should be clean and in good condition.

✓ Non-perishable food items should have at least 6 months remaining before expiration.

✓ Hygiene products should be unopened.

✓ Large furniture or appliance donations — please contact us first.

✓ All donations stay local and go directly into MLA programs.
