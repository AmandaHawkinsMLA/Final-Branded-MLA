Save One.

One step. One decision. One life changed — yours.

Save One is MLA's post-abortion healing class. A safe, confidential, faith-centered space for women ready to begin the journey toward wholeness, grace, and freedom.

"He heals the brokenhearted and binds up their wounds."Psalm 147:3

You don't have to carry this alone anymore.

Abortion is rarely talked about after it happens. Women are often left to grieve quietly — without language for what they're feeling, without community to hold them, and without a safe space to process the weight of it. Save One was built to change that.

This is not a class about guilt. It is not a class about politics. It is a class about healing — and the grace that is available to every woman who walks through the door.

Save One is open to any woman who has experienced abortion, regardless of how long ago it occurred or how she feels about it now. You do not have to have it figured out to show up.

What to Expect

  • A small, confidential group setting
  • Facilitated discussions — no pressure to share
  • Scripture-based content grounded in grace
  • Honest space for grief, anger, confusion, and hope
  • Connection with women who understand

What This Is Not

  • Not a debate about abortion
  • Not a place for judgment or shame
  • Not therapy (though we can connect you to counseling)
  • Not something you have to attend alone
The Journey

What healing actually looks like.

Healing is not linear. It is not a checklist or a five-step plan. Save One walks alongside women through the real, uneven, deeply human process of coming to peace with their story.

Acknowledgment

Naming what happened — without minimizing it, without dramatizing it, without qualifying it. Just the truth of your own experience, held gently.

Grief

Allowing yourself to feel what you've been carrying. Grief is not weakness. It is the evidence that something mattered. We make room for it here.

Forgiveness

Forgiving yourself is often the hardest part. Save One walks through what grace actually means — and what it looks like to receive it, not just believe it exists.

Restoration

Walking forward. Not as if it didn't happen — but as a whole person, with a story that includes both pain and redemption. That is the goal of Save One.

"Therefore, there is now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus."Romans 8:1

Ready to take the first step?

Save One classes are offered in small group format. Space is limited to protect the confidentiality and intimacy of the experience. If you are interested in joining an upcoming class or being notified when the next session opens, reach out to MLA directly.

Everything shared in Save One stays in Save One.
