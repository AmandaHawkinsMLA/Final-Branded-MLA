The Common Thread

Clothing with dignity. Community with heart.

Gently used and new clothing for children and adults of all ages — organized by size, season, and need. No paperwork. No applications. No references. Preserving dignity matters just as much as the clothes.

"She opens her arms to the poor and extends her hands to the needy."Proverbs 31:20

What we carry

The Common Thread is MLA's community clothing closet — a space where families and individuals can find what they need without an application process, a waiting list, or a means test. You need it. We have it. Come in.

Children's Clothing

Infant through teen, organized by size and season. School uniforms, play clothes, jackets, and shoes.

Adult Clothing

Men's and women's everyday wear, plus-size options, maternity, undergarments, coats, and accessories.

Workforce Ready

Interview-ready and professional clothing for anyone re-entering the workforce. Walk in with nothing. Walk out prepared.

Book an Appointment

No paperwork. No judgment.

We believe the way we give matters as much as what we give. The Common Thread is built around preserving dignity at every step.

By Appointment

Schedule a visit so we can give you dedicated time and personal attention — not a rushed experience.

By Referral

Community partners, schools, churches, and caseworkers can refer families directly. We coordinate with you.

Donate Clothing

Gently used clothing in good condition is always welcome. Drop off at 110 Jones Lane, Suite C, Flowood, MS.
