See Me Now

A story that began in Jackson, Mississippi.

Jameson's story sits at the bridge between the old era and the work being built now.

The moment.

In November 2021, Students for Life launched its See Me Now live ultrasound tour at the Mississippi State Capitol in Jackson before traveling toward Washington, D.C. ahead of the Dobbs hearing.

Jameson was part of that Jackson beginning. Today, his story continues not as a headline, but as a life still unfolding.

2021 — Jackson

The Ultrasound

Washington, D.C.

Now

Life kept unfolding. The story did not end when the headlines faded.
