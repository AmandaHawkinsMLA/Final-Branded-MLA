Foster Care Outreach

Rooted

Every child deserves roots.

Rooted is Mississippi Life Alliance's foster care outreach initiative — built to support children and families navigating the foster care system with dignity, practical resources, and consistent community presence.

No child should move through the system empty-handed.

Children in foster care often enter new homes with very little. Rooted exists to change that — providing backpacks, comfort items, school supplies, and encouragement to foster youth across Mississippi.

We also support families and teens aging out of the system, providing life-skill resources, interview clothing, and practical transition support through The Common Thread and our partner network.

Connect With Rooted Donate Supplies

Foster Youth Backpacks

Packed with essentials, comfort items, school supplies, and encouragement for children entering new placements.

Teen Transition Support

Life-skill resources, interview clothing, resume folders, and practical tools for teens aging out of foster care.

Comfort Kits

Small comfort items — stuffed animals, blankets, journals, and care notes — for children who need a soft landing.

Partner with Rooted.

Rooted grows through foster families, caseworkers, DFCS partners, churches, and individual donors who believe every child deserves to feel seen. If you work in the foster care system or want to support it, reach out.
