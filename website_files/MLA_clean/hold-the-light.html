Hold the Light.

Some lights have to be held for someone else.

You don't have to be certain. You don't have to have proof. You don't have to know what to call it. If something feels wrong about a child's situation — trust that. Say something. We'll take it from there.

If a child is in immediate danger

Call 911 now.

Do not wait. Do not submit a form. Call 911 immediately if a child is being hurt right now or is in immediate danger.

Mississippi Child Abuse Hotline

1-800-222-8000

Available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Anonymous reports accepted. Operated by the Mississippi Department of Child Protection Services.

National Child Abuse Hotline

1-800-422-4453

Childhelp National Child Abuse Hotline. 24/7. Confidential. Available in 170+ languages. Crisis intervention and referrals.

You noticed something. That matters.

Most people who witness or suspect child abuse don't report it — not because they don't care, but because they're not sure. They wonder if they're overreacting. They don't want to cause trouble. They hope someone else will say something.

But you're here. Which means you're not the someone else. You're the one.

MLA is not law enforcement and cannot remove a child from a home. What we can do is listen, document your concern, connect with the right people, and follow up — especially in situations where you know the family or want someone to gently check in before escalating.

"Speak up for those who cannot speak for themselves, for the rights of all who are destitute."Proverbs 31:8

What MLA does with your report

  • Every submission is read by staff
  • We assess and determine the appropriate next step
  • We can reach out to the family directly if you prefer that over a formal report
  • We connect with DCPS, school counselors, or other advocates as needed
  • We follow up with you if you leave contact info

What this form is for

The gray areas. The "I'm not sure if it's bad enough." The situations where you know the family and want a soft check-in before anything official. The things that have been sitting in the back of your mind. This is where you put them down.

Hold the Light

Tell us what you've seen.

This form is completely anonymous. You are never required to leave your name or contact information. If you do, we will only use it to follow up with you directly — never to identify you to anyone else.

Want us to follow up with you? (completely optional)

If you leave your contact information, we will only use it to update you on what steps MLA has taken. We will never share it or use it to identify you to the child's family or any agency.

By submitting this form you are sharing information with Mississippi Life Alliance staff only. MLA is not a mandated reporter agency — however, staff members who are licensed professionals may have independent legal obligations to report. If your concern rises to the level of immediate danger, please call 911 or the MS Child Abuse Hotline at 1-800-222-8000.
