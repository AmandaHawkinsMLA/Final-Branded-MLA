The Magnolia Society

Magnolia Rising

Every girl deserves her moment to bloom.

Magnolia Rising is the community pageant experience of The Magnolia Society — celebrating the character, poise, and potential of young women across Mississippi.

"She is more precious than rubies."Proverbs 31:10

More than a crown. A community.

Magnolia Rising is not your average pageant. It is a celebration of who these young women are becoming — built around character, community service, confidence, and the genuine warmth of Mississippi.

Participants are recognized not only for their presence but for the substance they carry: how they show up for others, how they lead, and how they bloom under pressure.

Coming Soon. Magnolia Rising is currently in development. Interest forms and registration information will be available soon. Contact us to express interest.

Character-First

The core of Magnolia Rising is character — demonstrated through service, leadership, and how participants carry themselves in community.

Community Rooted

This is a Mississippi pageant for Mississippi girls — celebrating local stories, local families, and local strength.

Open to All

Magnolia Rising is designed to be accessible and welcoming across backgrounds, sizes, abilities, and experience levels.
