Community

This is what community looks like.

Show up. Leave a prayer. Pass it on.

The MLA community board — prayer requests, local events, school drives, food drives, and the people and partners making Mississippi a little lighter every week.

Prayers on the Porch

Leave yours. We'll hold it.

Write down what's on your heart. Every request submitted here is printed, hung on the wall in our office, and prayed over by name. You are not carrying this alone. Leave your prayer. Take someone else's home with you.

"Bear one another's burdens, and so fulfill the law of Christ."Galatians 6:2

Your request will be printed and hung in the MLA office. If you'd like it kept completely private, note that in your message and we will honor it.

"Prayers on the Porch"

Our prayer wall is a real wall in our real office. Every submission gets printed, put up, and prayed over. When a prayer is answered, we celebrate it — and make room for the next one.

We read every single one.

Want to pray for others?

Come by the office and take a prayer off the wall to carry home with you. We'll have a take-one board at the front — anonymous requests available for anyone who wants to stand in the gap for a neighbor.

110 Jones Lane, Suite C · Flowood, MS

"The prayer of a righteous person is powerful and effective."James 5:16
