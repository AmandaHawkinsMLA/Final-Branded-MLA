Benevolence Ministry

Practical help for a pressing moment.

The MLA Benevolence Ministry exists to provide direct financial assistance to pregnant women and mothers facing urgent needs — because choosing life should never mean choosing between love and survival.

"Whoever is kind to the poor lends to the Lord, and he will reward them for what they have done."Proverbs 19:17

What the Benevolence Ministry covers.

We help cover the bills that stand between a family and stability. Assistance is direct, specific, and paid on behalf of the applicant to the provider.

Housing

Rent or mortgage payments. A lease agreement or landlord letter detailing the amount and payment information is required.

Utilities

Electricity, water, and gas bills. Copies of current bills are required at time of application.

Transportation

Car payments. Documentation of the payment amount and lender information required.

Eligibility

Who qualifies.

The Benevolence Ministry is designed specifically for women and families in a critical season of life.

Pregnancy or New Motherhood

Applicants must currently be pregnant or have given birth within the last two years.

Referral Required

All applications require a referral from a Pregnancy Resource Center, Embrace Grace group, church partner, or MLA board member.

Assistance Limits

Maximum of $1,000 per request. Applicants may not receive more than $1,000 in assistance within any six-month period. Deposits and partial payments are not covered.

How to Apply

What you will need.

Step 1 — Get a Referral

Contact your pregnancy resource center, Embrace Grace group, church, or an MLA board member to receive a referral before applying.

Step 2 — Gather Your Documents

You will need: a valid state ID or driver's license, and copies of all bills you are requesting assistance with. For rent, include your lease or a letter from your landlord.

Step 3 — Complete the Application

Submit the application below with your referral information and all required documents. Incomplete applications cannot be processed.

Step 4 — We Follow Up

Our team will review your application and follow up directly. Payments are made on your behalf to the provider — not directly to applicants.

Benevolence Application

You will be asked to upload your ID and bill copies when our team follows up. Please have them ready.

For urgent help or questions, call us at (601) 956-8636 or email ahawkins@mslifealliance.org.
