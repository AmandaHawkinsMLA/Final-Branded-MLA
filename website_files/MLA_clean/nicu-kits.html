Wellness Baskets

NICU Kits & Wellness Baskets

Practical care, thoughtfully packed.

Our wellness baskets are curated for moments that matter — newborns in the NICU, mothers during pregnancy and postpartum, outreach to people experiencing homelessness, and families rebuilding with dignity.

Root Bound
NICU & Newborn Support
"Before I formed you in the womb I knew you."Jeremiah 1:5

Designed for families with infants in the NICU — small comforts and practical items for parents and newborns.

  • Newborn clothing, receiving blankets, and swaddles
  • Diapers (newborn size), wipes, and diaper cream
  • Comfort items for parents: socks, warm beverage packets, journals
  • Nursing supplies and lactation support resources
  • Encouragement cards and local resource contact card
Bloom Bound
Prenatal Kits
"You knit me together in my mother's womb."Psalm 139:13–14

Packed to support expectant mothers through pregnancy with comfort and practical items.

  • Prenatal vitamins reminder card and wellness tips
  • Comfort items: water bottle, compression socks, snacks
  • Hygiene and self-care essentials
  • Resource navigation card and local support contacts
Covered
Postpartum Support
"She is clothed in strength and dignity."Proverbs 31:25

Support for the immediate postpartum period emphasizing rest and recovery.

  • Postpartum hygiene kits and comfort items
  • Snacks, hydration, and easy-care meals
  • Encouragement journal and devotional
  • Breastfeeding/nursing supplies and storage bags
Open Table
Homeless Outreach Kits
"I was hungry and you gave me food."Matthew 25:35

Ready-to-go outreach kits for people experiencing homelessness — practical, dignity-centered, and portable.

  • Hygiene essentials, toothbrush, toothpaste, soap
  • Socks, gloves, and weather-appropriate items
  • Non-perishable snacks, water, and small first-aid items
  • Resource card with local shelter and service info
Branch Out
Community Transition Kits
"Carry one another's burdens."Galatians 6:2

Kits to help families transition after a crisis — cleaning supplies, basic kitchenware, and starter items for a home.

  • Basic cookware, utensils, and plates
  • Cleaning supplies and laundry detergent
  • Bedding basics: pillowcase, blanket
  • Information on local support services and case management

How to request a kit

If you or someone you know needs a kit, please request help or contact MLA for referral options. Medical partners and hospitals can request NICU Kits by emailing ahawkins@mslifealliance.org.
