Wellness Baskets

Curated care for every season of life.

Each basket is built around a moment — a birth, a new beginning, a hard night, a fresh start. Every item inside is chosen with intention.

Root Bound
For Our Littlest Magnolias
"Before I formed you in the womb I knew you."Jeremiah 1:5

For the newest arrivals — NICU and newborn care packages for mom and baby during fragile beginnings.

  • Newborn essentials & diapers
  • Comfy socks, robes & blankets
  • Journals, pens & water bottles
  • Chapstick, lotion & encouragement cards
  • Snacks, gift cards & nursing supplies
  • Small devotionals & breast milk storage bags
Bloom Bound
Prenatal Kits
"You knit me together in my mother's womb. I praise you because I am fearfully and wonderfully made."Psalm 139:13–14

For expectant mothers carrying the next generation forward — practical comfort and care through every stage of pregnancy.

  • Prenatal vitamins reminder card & wellness items
  • Comfort essentials & hygiene products
  • Encouragement notes & devotional
  • Snacks & self-care items
  • Resource navigation card
Covered
Postpartum Support
"She is clothed in strength and dignity."Proverbs 31:25

A postpartum care basket built around rest, recovery, and the reminder that she is not doing this alone.

  • Postpartum hygiene essentials
  • Nursing or bottle-feeding supplies
  • Comforting snacks & warm socks
  • Encouragement journal & devotional
  • Self-care items & resource card
Open Table
Homeless Outreach
"I was hungry and you gave me food, I was thirsty and you gave me drink, I was a stranger and you invited me in."Matthew 25:35

No one should face the night alone. Open Table outreach kits are built for people experiencing homelessness — practical, dignity-centered, and ready to go.

  • Hygiene products & travel-size toiletries
  • Socks, gloves & weather items
  • Non-perishable snacks & water
  • Blanket or emergency poncho
  • Resource information card
