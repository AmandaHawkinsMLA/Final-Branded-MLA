Adoption in Mississippi.

Every child deserves a family. Every family deserves support.

Adoption is a profound act of love — for the child, for the birth family, and for the adoptive family. MLA provides education, resources, and connection for every part of the adoption journey.

"God sets the lonely in families."Psalm 68:6

Adoption is not a last resort. It is a first love.

In a culture that often frames adoption as a consolation prize — either for the child who "wasn't wanted" or the family who "couldn't have their own" — MLA holds a different view. Adoption is a deliberate, courageous act of love. It is a birth mother making an extraordinary sacrifice. It is a family opening their home. It is a child being chosen.

It is not simple. It is not painless. And it is worth knowing the truth about what it involves.

Types of Adoption

  • Domestic Infant Adoption — Working with an agency or attorney to adopt a newborn placed by a birth parent in the U.S.
  • Foster-to-Adopt — Becoming a licensed foster family with the intention of adopting if parental rights are terminated
  • International Adoption — Adopting a child from another country through an accredited agency and the U.S. immigration process
  • Relative (Kinship) Adoption — A family member legally adopting a relative's child
  • Stepparent Adoption — A stepparent legally adopting their spouse's child

In Mississippi, the Department of Child Protection Services (DCPS) oversees foster care and adoption. The state has an ongoing need for licensed foster and adoptive families, particularly for sibling groups, older children, and children with special needs.

For Birth Families

If you are considering placing a child for adoption.

Choosing adoption for your child is one of the most selfless decisions a person can make. It is also one of the most complex. You deserve honest information, unhurried support, and someone who will walk with you — without pressure, without agenda, without judgment.

You have the right to choose.

No one should pressure you in any direction. Adoption is a choice — made freely, with full information. MLA believes in informed, empowered decision-making for every birth parent.

Open adoption is an option.

Many adoptions today are "open" or "semi-open" — meaning birth parents may maintain contact with their child after placement. Arrangements vary and are typically documented in an agreement.

You can change your mind.

In Mississippi, a birth parent can revoke consent to adoption within a legally defined window after placement. Speak with an attorney or adoption professional to understand your rights.

MLA can connect you with trusted adoption agencies, licensed counselors, and legal resources. We are not an adoption agency — we are a support network. Contact us to talk to someone.
