Community Pantry

The Porchlight Commons

Serving Mississippians since '26 ♡

A community pantry built around practical food support, emergency staples, and meal help for families and individuals across Mississippi.

"The generous will themselves be blessed, for they share their food with the poor."Proverbs 22:9

What we provide

The Porchlight Commons is the food and pantry arm of Mississippi Life Alliance — a stocked resource for families in need of non-perishable staples, snack support, meal assistance, and emergency food help.

Pantry Staples

Canned goods, pasta, rice, oatmeal, peanut butter, cereal, crackers, and shelf-stable staples.

Snack Packs

Pre-packed snack bags for children and families — ready to go, easy to carry, and built to fill the gap.

Meal Support

Frozen meals, restaurant gift cards, grocery store gift cards, and individually wrapped snacks for emergencies.

Food Rescue

Growing partnerships with restaurants, hotels, and grocery providers to redirect surplus food directly to families in need.

The Porchlight Commons runs on community generosity. Non-perishable food, grocery gift cards, and pantry donations make the difference between a family eating tonight or going without.
