Light Keepers

Be the light that keeps going.

Volunteers at Mississippi Life Alliance are called Light Keepers — people who choose to show up, stay consistent, and keep the porch light burning for someone who needs it.

Sort + Stock

Help organize donations, stock The Common Thread closet, prepare Wellness Baskets, and assemble outreach supplies.

Deliver + Pick Up

Help pick up donations, deliver resources to schools and families, and move supplies where they are needed most.

Professional Light Keepers

Nurses, counselors, tutors, advocates, social workers, admin helpers, drivers, photographers, and event support.

"In the same way, let your light shine before others, that they may see your good deeds and glorify your Father in heaven."Matthew 5:16
